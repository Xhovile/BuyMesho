# Checkpoint 5 — Seller Commitments & Marketplace Standards

## Purpose
Establish the commitments a seller accepts before BuyMesho approves marketplace selling capability.

## Seller commitments
### Product authenticity
The seller agrees to:
- Post authentic products.
- Avoid scams and fraudulent listings.
- Avoid misleading buyers.
- Accurately represent products, prices, quantities, and conditions.

### BuyMesho commission
The seller accepts:
- 3% commission on every successful sale.
- Applicable PayChangu / payment-processing transaction fees where they apply.

### Product presentation
Listings should use clean, clear product images. Preferred presentation backgrounds include white, pink, black, grey, or other clean/bright backgrounds that keep the product easy to understand.

## Acceptance criteria
- Seller standards are shown before approval.
- Seller explicitly accepts the applicable commitments.
- Listing and moderation systems can enforce marketplace standards.
- Commission and transaction-fee rules are represented consistently across seller and transaction flows.

## Implementation note
This checkpoint defines marketplace rules; it does not require every seller to offer special services or student deals. Those are covered by later checkpoints.
