# Checkpoint 6 — Student Deal Commitment

## Purpose
Create a continuing commercial relationship between BuyMesho and public/non-student sellers through recurring student-focused offers.

## Product rule
A Public / Non-Student seller should commit to offering a student deal for at least two weeks every six months.

## Expected behavior
- Seller identifies and submits the applicable student deal or promotion.
- BuyMesho records the commitment and its validity period.
- Student-facing marketplace surfaces can identify eligible student deals.
- BuyMesho can monitor whether the seller has met the recurring commitment.

## Scope
This rule applies to Public / Non-Student sellers as defined by the seller profile. It does not turn every listing into a student-only listing.

## Acceptance criteria
- The commitment is presented during applicable seller onboarding or agreement flows.
- Deal periods have explicit start and end dates.
- The system can distinguish an active student deal from an expired one.
- The requirement does not prevent public sellers from serving the wider marketplace.

## Future policy note
The exact enforcement, exemptions, and consequences for missed commitments should be defined before automated enforcement is introduced.
