# Checkpoint 8 — Unified Marketplace Structure

## Purpose
Keep BuyMesho as one marketplace for students, public sellers, and businesses instead of creating separate student and public marketplaces.

## Structure
Marketplace categories may include:
- Fashion
- Electronics
- Phones
- Beauty
- Food
- Agriculture
- Services
- Other supported categories

Each category can contain products from:
- Student sellers
- Public sellers
- Businesses

## Rules
- Seller type must not determine marketplace category membership.
- Student-facing capabilities should be an additional layer over the common marketplace.
- Buyers should discover products through the same core marketplace experience.
- Student deals and eligibility can provide additional filtering/promotion without splitting inventory into a separate marketplace.

## Acceptance criteria
- Category pages can show products from all approved seller types.
- Seller type is available as useful metadata/filtering where appropriate, not as a hard marketplace partition.
- Student-specific offers remain additive to the common marketplace.
- Search, category browsing, recommendations, cart, checkout, orders, and messaging operate across the unified marketplace.
