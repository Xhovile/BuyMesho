# Checkpoint 4 — Seller Information

## Purpose
Make seller onboarding capture meaningful marketplace information without polluting general account registration.

## Seller information
A seller should tell BuyMesho:
- What they intend to sell.
- Why they want to sell on BuyMesho.
- Whether they are a Student or Public seller.
- Whether they offer additional services such as Lay-by or financing, where applicable.
- Any business or operational information required for the selected seller capability.

## Rules
- Information must be relevant to the seller's actual activity.
- Student-specific fields apply only to student sellers.
- Public sellers remain ordinary public users in the marketplace.
- Optional seller capabilities should not become universal requirements.

## Acceptance criteria
- Seller application data gives BuyMesho enough context to review the seller.
- Seller type is consistent with the general account classification.
- Additional services can be declared without forcing them on sellers who do not offer them.
- The seller can later maintain appropriate business information through seller settings.
