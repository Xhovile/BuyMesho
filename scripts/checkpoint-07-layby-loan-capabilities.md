# Checkpoint 7 — Lay-by / Loan Seller Capabilities

## Purpose
Treat Lay-by and loan/financing offers as optional seller capabilities with additional marketplace standards.

## Seller requirements
Sellers offering these services must:
- Provide legitimate offers.
- Clearly state the terms and conditions.
- State applicable percentages, fees, schedules, and obligations transparently.
- Use reasonable and flexible percentages consistent with BuyMesho rules.
- Avoid excessive charges that could damage buyer trust or the BuyMesho ecosystem.
- Operate within BuyMesho's marketplace rules for these services.

## Product model
Lay-by and loans are seller capabilities, not requirements for every seller.

## Acceptance criteria
- Sellers can declare whether they provide these services.
- Special-service terms are shown clearly to buyers before commitment.
- BuyMesho can distinguish ordinary product sales from financing/lay-by offers.
- Applicable moderation and policy controls can review these offers.

## Policy dependency
Before production enforcement, BuyMesho should define the exact permitted percentages, disclosures, eligibility rules, dispute handling, and any legal/compliance requirements.
